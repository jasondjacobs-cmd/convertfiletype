How it works

PNG to AVIF in three steps

  1. Choose a PNG image.The file stays on your device and transparent pixels are retained.
  2. Choose AVIF quality.80% is the default balance between image quality and output size.
  3. Download the AVIF.The AVIF is encoded locally in your browser using ConvertFileType's self-hosted encoder.

Privacy

Your image does not get uploaded to us.

The conversion runs in your browser using a same-origin AVIF WebAssembly encoder. ConvertFileType does not receive or store your source PNG or generated AVIF.
